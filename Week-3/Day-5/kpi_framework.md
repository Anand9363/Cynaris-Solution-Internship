
# W3D5 - KPI Framework
## Domain: E-Commerce / Retail Analytics

| KPI | Formula | Owner | Frequency | Target | Type |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Total Revenue | SUM(price) | Sales Manager | Monthly | Grow 10% | Lagging |
| Total Orders | COUNT DISTINCT(order_id) | Operations Manager | Monthly | Grow 10% | Lagging |
| Products Sold | COUNT(order items) | Sales Manager | Monthly | Grow 10% | Lagging |
| Average Order Value | Revenue / Orders | Business Analyst | Monthly | Grow 5% | Lagging |
| Average Product Price | AVG(price) | Category Manager | Monthly | Monitor baseline | Lagging |
| Total Freight | SUM(freight_value) | Logistics Manager | Monthly | Monitor cost | Lagging |
| Average Freight | AVG(freight_value) | Logistics Manager | Monthly | Reduce 3% | Lagging |
| Active Sellers | DISTINCT(seller_id) | Seller Manager | Monthly | Maintain baseline | Lagging |
| Unique Products | DISTINCT(product_id) | Category Manager | Monthly | Maintain baseline | Lagging |
| Revenue per Seller | Revenue / Active Sellers | Sales Manager | Monthly | Grow 5% | Lagging |

*Targets are proposed improvements against an established baseline.*

## Business Insights

1. Total revenue generated is Rs. 1.36 crore.
2. The dataset contains 98,666 unique orders.
3. There are 3,095 active sellers.
4. Average order value is Rs. 137.75.
5. Total freight cost is Rs. 22.52 lakh.
6. Freight represents approximately 16.57% of item revenue.
7. The highest-revenue seller generated Rs. 2.29 lakh.

## Conclusion

The KPI analysis provides an overview of e-commerce
sales, orders, products, sellers and freight costs.

The business can use these KPIs to monitor sales
performance and identify opportunities for improvement.

Monthly comparisons are required to identify trends.
